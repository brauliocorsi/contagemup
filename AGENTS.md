Picking membership in Contagem is resolved through `loadProductResolver` and carried as `PickingLine.registered`; this keeps the on-screen, printed, and exported status consistent without removing unmatched ERP lines.
Product list headers and virtual rows share one scroll container and the same column widths, including fixed controls, to prevent alignment drift when columns are hidden.
Product orphan badges use the trigger-maintained `colis_orfaos` value rather than deriving stock from the latest counting session, which may contain only part of current inventory.- Global (category/keyword) audits use scope='global' with item location 'TODAS'; complete_location_audit only applies items whose resolution is 'ajustar' (global shortages debit unlocated stock first, excess goes to CONF) — keeps divergence decisions explicit.
